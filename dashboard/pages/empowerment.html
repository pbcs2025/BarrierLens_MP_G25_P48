Female Autonomy & Empowerment Insights

Female autonomy indicators are strongly associated with reduced healthcare barrier vulnerability in India. Women possessing all three autonomy assets (bank account, mobile phone, and healthcare decision agency) exhibit an overall barrier rate of 52.67%, compared to 59.32% among those with zero autonomy indicators. In particular, household financial and permission constraints drop from 27.34% to 20.91%, demonstrating the protective buffering role of female financial inclusion.
