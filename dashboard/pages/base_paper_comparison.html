          Benchmark Discrepancy & Alignment Notes

          The base paper (Pradhan & De, 2025) reports an overall barrier rate of 84.0%, whereas BarrierLens observed rate across the complete NFHS-5 individual recode extract (N = 724,115) is 60.68%. Both frameworks consistently rank Facility constraints as the predominant barrier, followed by Logistic/distance friction and Household financial barriers. Discrepancies in absolute magnitude are attributable to differences in subsample filtering and question recoding definitions.
