Demographic Gradient Insights

Demographic analysis demonstrates steep gradients in healthcare access barriers across socio-economic status. Women in the poorest wealth quintile experience 75.6% barrier rates compared to 39.5% among the richest quintile. Similarly, uneducated and rural agricultural workers face compounded barrier risks across facility, distance, and household financial autonomy dimensions.
