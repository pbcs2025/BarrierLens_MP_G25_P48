Key Demographic Insights

Across all five demographic dimensions surveyed in NFHS-5 (Wealth, Residence, Education, Age, and Occupation), healthcare access barriers display steep gradient patterns. Barriers to healthcare access are significantly higher among poor, uneducated, rural, and agricultural women. Poorest women face a 75.6% prevalence of healthcare access barriers compared to 39.5% among the richest quintile, highlighting pronounced socioeconomic gradient effects.
