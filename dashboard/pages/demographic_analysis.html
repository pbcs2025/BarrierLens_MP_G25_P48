Demographic Gradient Insights

Demographic analysis demonstrates sharp gradients in healthcare access constraints across socioeconomic status. Women in the poorest wealth quintile experience 75.6% barrier rates compared to 39.5% among the richest quintile (a 1.91x disparity). Similarly, women without formal schooling and those engaged in rural agricultural labor face compounded vulnerabilities across facility medicine shortages and transportation barriers.
