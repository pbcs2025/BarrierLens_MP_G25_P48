Regional Disparity & State Insights

State-level analysis reveals substantial geographical heterogeneity across India's 36 States and Union Territories. Facility-level barriers remain the dominant constraint in the vast majority of high-prevalence states, whereas household financial and decision-making barriers vary significantly by state socioeconomic indicators. Machine learning composite risk scores (Rail B) strongly correlate with observed state-level vulnerability rates.
