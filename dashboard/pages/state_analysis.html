Geographical Insights & Regional Variation

State-level analysis indicates significant geographical variation in barrier prevalence across India. Observed differences are characterized by higher logistic and facility rates in mountainous and northeastern territories, while southern and western states reflect lower composite barrier indicators. Observed rankings reflect cross-sectional survey sample distributions in NFHS-5 and should not be used to make political or causal assertions.
