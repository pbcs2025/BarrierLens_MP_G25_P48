Geographical Insights & Spatial Disparity

State-level analysis reveals severe regional inequalities across India's 36 States and Union Territories. States such as Chhattisgarh, Arunachal Pradesh, and Nagaland exhibit the highest combined barrier burden, driven primarily by facility-level constraints and terrain-based logistic barriers. Conversely, Southern and Western states like Kerala and Goa demonstrate lower barrier rates due to denser healthcare infrastructure and higher female literacy.
