Executive National Insights

Across 724,115 Indian women surveyed in NFHS-5, healthcare access barriers remain widespread, with 60.68% experiencing at least one major constraint. Facility-level concerns represent the primary constraint (46.01% observed), followed by logistic barriers (31.61%) and household financial/autonomy constraints (27.16%). BarrierLens machine learning predictions closely match this domain hierarchy, demonstrating consistent macro-level alignment across observed and model outputs.
