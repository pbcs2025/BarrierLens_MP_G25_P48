Executive Insights & National Trends

Across 724,115 Indian women surveyed in NFHS-5, healthcare access barriers remain highly prevalent, with 60.68% experiencing at least one major barrier. Facility-level concerns represent the primary constraint (46.01% observed), followed by logistic barriers (31.61%) and household financial/autonomy constraints (27.16%). BarrierLens machine learning predictions closely match this rank ordering, confirming macro-level alignment across observed and predicted domains.
