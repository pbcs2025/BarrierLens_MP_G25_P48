Rural–Urban Insights & Disparities

Rural women show higher healthcare-access barriers across all three domains compared to urban women (63.49% vs 46.03% facing at least one barrier). Facility-level concerns represent the dominant barrier domain for both groups (49.18% in rural vs 36.38% in urban settings). Logistic barriers exhibit the highest relative disparity ratio (1.38x higher in rural areas), reflecting infrastructure and transportation gaps in rural healthcare access.
