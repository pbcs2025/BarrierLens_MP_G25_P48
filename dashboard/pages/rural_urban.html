Rural-Urban Residence Disparity Insights

Place of residence represents one of the most critical structural determinants of healthcare access in India. Rural women experience significantly higher overall barrier prevalence (63.49% vs 46.03%), with logistic and distance constraints showing the largest relative gap (1.8x higher risk in rural areas due to transportation infrastructure deficits).
