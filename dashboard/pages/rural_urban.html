Residence Disparity Insights

Place of residence represents a decisive structural determinant of healthcare access in India. Rural women face significantly higher overall barrier prevalence (63.49% vs 46.03%), with transportation and distance barriers exhibiting the largest relative disparity (1.57x higher in rural areas). While facility constraints remain the single highest barrier in both environments, rural populations face compounded friction due to physical distance from tertiary care centers.
