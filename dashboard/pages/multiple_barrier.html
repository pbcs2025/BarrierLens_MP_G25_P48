Compounded Disadvantage Insights

The coexistence of household, logistic, and facility-level barriers highlights compounded disadvantage among vulnerable women — nearly 1 in 3 Indian women (30.85%) faces 2 or more concurrent healthcare access barriers. Severe multi-barrier exposure is disproportionately concentrated in the poorest wealth quintile (45.2% facing 2+ barriers) compared to the richest quintile (14.8%).
