Multiple Barrier Compounded Disadvantage Insights

Multiple barrier analysis reveals severe intersectional vulnerability among economically disadvantaged women. While only 13.9% of richest quintile women face ≥2 concurrent barriers, nearly half (49.1%) of women in the poorest wealth quintile experience multiple overlapping constraints. In rural communities, 35.8% experience ≥2 barriers compared to only 18.7% in urban centers, demonstrating that structural healthcare deficits tend to compound rather than occur in isolation.
