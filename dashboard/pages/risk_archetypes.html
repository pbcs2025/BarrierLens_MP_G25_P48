Research Interpretation & Policy Implications

The K-Means clustering algorithm ($k=2$, Silhouette Score $0.3986$) bifurcates the national population into two distinct behavioral archetypes. Cluster 0 (52.9% of women) exhibits severe structural disadvantage: low media penetration (0.349), limited digital literacy (0.079), and high vulnerability scores (0.769), which correspond to high predicted barrier probabilities across Household (59.1%), Logistic (59.6%), and Facility (57.4%) domains. Conversely, Cluster 1 (47.1% of women) benefits from high media exposure (0.915) and reduced vulnerability (0.262), translating to 20+ percentage-point reductions in predicted healthcare access barriers. Interventions aiming to increase healthcare utilization must specifically target Cluster 0 through localized, non-digital outreach strategies.
