Stage 2 Predictive Insights

Stage 2 evaluations demonstrate that incorporating Stage 1 barrier probabilities provides statistically consistent incremental predictive power for secondary maternal health deficits. For Unmet Family Planning Need (N = 466,859), the full model reaches an AUC of 0.6591 (+0.0017 uplift). For Antenatal Care Deficits (<4 visits, N = 163,018), the full model achieves an AUC of 0.6356 (+0.0019 uplift) over demographic baselines.
