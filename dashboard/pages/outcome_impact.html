Research Synthesis & Metric Honest Interpretation

Stage 2 empirical results demonstrate that adding predicted access barrier probabilities yields small but positive incremental predictive performance over baseline socioeconomic predictors ($+0.0017$ ROC-AUC uplift for Unmet FP; $+0.0020$ ROC-AUC uplift for ANC Gap). While household barrier probabilities emerge as significant individual risk factors ($\text{OR} = 1.15$ for Unmet FP, $\text{OR} = 1.31$ for ANC Gap), underlying socioeconomic determinants (wealth quintile, literacy, place of residence) capture the vast majority of predictive variance. In accordance with rigorous scientific standards, these uplift metrics are presented accurately without overstatement.
