Stage 2 Utilization Insights

Stage 2 predictive models demonstrate that Stage 1 barrier probabilities provide modest, statistically consistent incremental value when predicting downstream health service gaps. For Unmet Family Planning Need (N = 466,859), the full model achieves an AUC of 0.6591 (+0.0017 over socio-demographic baseline). For Antenatal Care Gap (N = 163,018), the full model achieves an AUC of 0.6356 (+0.0019 over baseline).
