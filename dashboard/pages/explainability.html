Academic Interpretation & Non-Causal Guidance

How to interpret feature attribution: Feature contributions and SHAP values indicate how each input variable influenced the machine learning model's output for an individual woman. This describes model decision behavior and empirical predictive associations. In cross-sectional observational survey data such as NFHS-5, these associations do not establish real-world physical or medical causation.

Key Explainability Findings

Poorest wealth status (v190: Poorest) consistently ranks among the most potent risk factors across all three Stage 1 barrier targets. Conversely, richest wealth and bank account ownership act as consistent protective mitigating factors. Tree-based SHAP summary distributions confirm that socioeconomic standing, place of residence, and autonomy permissions account for over 65% of aggregate predictive attribution.
