💡 KEY INSIGHTS (NFHS-5 Empirical Grounding)
📊 Rural vs. Urban Disparity
Rural women (75.2% sample) face a +14.6 pp higher barrier burden compared to urban counterparts, primarily driven by travel distance and transportation unavailability.
🏥 Most Prevalent Barrier
Facility-level issues (provider quality, absence of female health providers, medicine stockouts) affect 46.01% of all surveyed women nationally.
💰 Socioeconomic Gradient
Poorest quintile women experience a 78.4% barrier rate vs 43.8% in the richest quintile—an empirical disparity ratio of 1.79x.
🛡️ Compound Vulnerability
Over 31.55% of respondents face ≥2 concurrent overlapping barriers spanning financial, spatial, and institutional dimensions.
